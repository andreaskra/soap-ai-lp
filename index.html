07 · The honest questions

What every owner asks on the first call.

"My business is different."
That's exactly why we don't sell generic AI tools. Every build is shaped around how your specific business runs. Your clients, your pricing logic, your SOPs. Generic tools give generic outputs. We build around how you actually work.
"I don't have time to implement this."
We need 2–3 hours of your time in week one. After that we run the build. The system gives you time back, it does not cost it. If you can't find 2 hours in a fortnight, this isn't the right moment.
"What if my team won't use it?"
They don't have to. The AI layer runs in the background of the tools your team already uses. Email, your CRM, your accounting system. We don't ask anyone to adopt a new app. That adoption problem is the whole reason most software fails.
"Is my data safe?"
Yes. We work inside your existing systems. Your data does not leave your environment. We sign whatever your lawyer wants signed.

Because the audit puts a dollar number on the leaks before you sign anything. If we identify $300K of annual leaks and the build costs $20K, the case is made on paper. If we can't find leaks worth more than the build, we tell you straight and refer you elsewhere.

Good. Neither are most of our clients. The whole point of SOAP is that you stay the operator and we run the AI side. You'll never touch a line of code, configure a tool, or learn a new piece of software. You ask questions, we run the build.
